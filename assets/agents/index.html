Regional Finance loan flow v1 · agent-readable copy

A plain-HTML copy of the v1 loan flow prototype for AI agents and reviewers. Each journey lists its screens in order; each screen page says what the screen is for and shows what the customer sees (headings, copy, fields, buttons, legal text). Demo data only (“Jordan Rivera”, “Test User”).

How the prototype is organized

  • Known customer prequal (Nov 2026 release): a known customer with a pre-qualified offer starts on a channel: an affiliate’s page (MoneyLion, Credit Karma, LendingTree, NerdWallet, AmONE), ChatGPT (a concept, not in production) or Regional’s own email or text. Affiliate and ChatGPT pages are prototypes of pages Regional does not host; they are out of scope. Every channel then opens the same Regional pre-qualified offer landing and the same funnel: SMS code → Is this you? → bank verification → credit authorization → processing → funding account → sign → funded.
  • New customer: new borrower landing → About you (3 pages) → confirm → SMS code → soft-pull consent → offer → the shared funnel.
  • Known customer and re-entry: the existing flows.

A concept, not in production: the customer asks ChatGPT about credit card debt, ChatGPT suggests Regional, collects the basics, Regional’s secure form takes date of birth, SSN and consents, and ChatGPT shows Regional’s offers. Continuing opens Regional’s pre-qualified offer landing; from there the funnel is the same for every channel.
  • Known customer prequal · Regional email or text (no affiliate) — 13 screens. Regional emails or texts a known customer a pre-qualified offer link. The customer starts on Regional’s pre-qualified offer landing.
  • Known customer (existing flow) — 16 screens. An existing Regional customer confirms the phone on file, enters a code, confirms their details, gives the soft-pull consent, picks an offer and continues into the shared flow.
  • New customer — 19 screens. A new customer starts on the new borrower landing, fills in the three About-you pages, confirms their information and mobile number, enters a code, gives the soft-pull consent, picks an offer and continues into the shared flow.
  • Re-entry customer — 13 screens. A customer returning to finish an application they started confirms their phone, enters a code and goes back to their offer.
