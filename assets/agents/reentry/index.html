Re-entry customer

A customer returning to finish an application they started confirms their phone, enters a code and goes back to their offer.

13 screens, in order:

  1. Re-entry: confirm phone — Returning customer: confirm the phone on file to continue the application.
  2. SMS code — Enter the 6-digit code texted to the customer. (Demo: a simulated text notification offers “Tap to prefill”; code 429833.)
  3. Offer — The pre-qualified offer (existing page): amount ($1,000–$15,000), term, origination fee, APR, monthly payment, autopay. Prequal customers come here straight from “Is this you?” (no Check-for-offers step: the offer already exists).
  4. Bank verification — Bank verification: why we link a bank. (Simulated; to be replaced by Plaid.)
  5. Pick a bank — Pick a bank (simulated bank link).
  6. Bank sign-in — Sign in to the bank (simulated; demo credentials only).
  7. Connecting — Connecting to the bank (animated).
  8. Bank connected — Bank connected.
  9. Credit authorization (hard pull) — Final credit authorization (hard pull).
  10. Processing — Loader while the application is processed.
  11. Choose funding account — Approved: choose the account the money goes to.
  12. Sign the agreement — Review and sign the loan agreement.
  13. Funded — Funded confirmation: the money has been sent, disclosures, portal login.
