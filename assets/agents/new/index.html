New customer

A new customer starts on the new borrower landing, fills in the three About-you pages, confirms their information and mobile number, enters a code, gives the soft-pull consent, picks an offer and continues into the shared flow.

19 screens, in order:

  1. New borrower landing — New borrower landing (regionalfinance.com patterns): why Regional, 3 steps to funded, a personal approach, reviews, approved legal footer. “Check your offers” opens the About-you pages.
  2. About you 1/3 — About you 1 of 3: name and date of birth.
  3. About you 2/3 — About you 2 of 3: home address (simulated autocomplete) and home ownership.
  4. About you 3/3 — About you 3 of 3: annual income and Social Security number.
  5. Confirm your information — Confirm the information entered and the mobile number for the code.
  6. SMS code — Enter the 6-digit code texted to the customer. (Demo: a simulated text notification offers “Tap to prefill”; code 429833.)
  7. Soft-pull consent — Soft-pull consent before checking for offers (no credit impact).
  8. Checking for offers — Loader while offers are checked.
  9. Offer — The pre-qualified offer (existing page): amount ($1,000–$15,000), term, origination fee, APR, monthly payment, autopay. Prequal customers come here straight from “Is this you?” (no Check-for-offers step: the offer already exists).
  10. Bank verification — Bank verification: why we link a bank. (Simulated; to be replaced by Plaid.)
  11. Pick a bank — Pick a bank (simulated bank link).
  12. Bank sign-in — Sign in to the bank (simulated; demo credentials only).
  13. Connecting — Connecting to the bank (animated).
  14. Bank connected — Bank connected.
  15. Credit authorization (hard pull) — Final credit authorization (hard pull).
  16. Processing — Loader while the application is processed.
  17. Choose funding account — Approved: choose the account the money goes to.
  18. Sign the agreement — Review and sign the loan agreement.
  19. Funded — Funded confirmation: the money has been sent, disclosures, portal login.
