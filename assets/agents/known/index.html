Known customer (existing flow)

An existing Regional customer confirms the phone on file, enters a code, confirms their details, gives the soft-pull consent, picks an offer and continues into the shared flow.

16 screens, in order:

  1. Confirm phone — Confirm the phone number on file before a code is texted.
  2. SMS code — Enter the 6-digit code texted to the customer. (Demo: a simulated text notification offers “Tap to prefill”; code 429833.)
  3. Is this you? — “Is this you?” — the details Regional has on file, to confirm.
  4. Soft-pull consent — Soft-pull consent before checking for offers (no credit impact).
  5. Checking for offers — Loader while offers are checked.
  6. Offer — The pre-qualified offer: amount ($1,000–$15,000), term, APR, monthly payment, autopay.
  7. Bank verification — Bank verification: why we link a bank. (Simulated; to be replaced by Plaid.)
  8. Pick a bank — Pick a bank (simulated bank link).
  9. Bank sign-in — Sign in to the bank (simulated; demo credentials only).
  10. Connecting — Connecting to the bank (animated).
  11. Bank connected — Bank connected.
  12. Credit authorization (hard pull) — Final credit authorization (hard pull).
  13. Processing — Loader while the application is processed.
  14. Choose funding account — Approved: choose the account the money goes to.
  15. Sign the agreement — Review and sign the loan agreement.
  16. Funded — Funded confirmation: the money has been sent, disclosures, portal login.
