Known customer prequal · NerdWallet

A known customer is matched with Regional on NerdWallet. The page before Regional’s landing is the affiliate’s own page (a prototype of it, hosted by the affiliate, out of scope for Regional). Tapping Regional’s offer there opens Regional’s pre-qualified offer landing; from there the funnel is the same for every channel.

15 screens, in order:

  1. Affiliate page — The affiliate’s page (prototype, hosted by the affiliate, out of scope for Regional). Regional supplies the offer; layout, ratings and badges are the affiliate’s.
  2. Pre-qualified offer landing — Regional’s pre-qualified offer landing (Nov 2026 release). The pre-qualified offer is shown ($15,000, 36 months, 13.01% APR); the customer can change amount, term and autopay later, on the existing offer page after “Is this you?”. The customer accepts the consents in the pinned bar and continues. When the channel sends no usable phone (Credit Karma) it also asks for a mobile number, and the code is texted there. Next: the SMS code.
  3. SMS code — Enter the 6-digit code texted to the customer. (Demo: a simulated text notification offers “Tap to prefill”; code 429833.)
  4. Is this you? — “Is this you?” — the details Regional has on file, to confirm.
  5. Offer — The pre-qualified offer (existing page): amount ($1,000–$15,000), term, origination fee, APR, monthly payment, autopay. Prequal customers come here straight from “Is this you?” (no Check-for-offers step: the offer already exists).
  6. Bank verification — Bank verification: why we link a bank. (Simulated; to be replaced by Plaid.)
  7. Pick a bank — Pick a bank (simulated bank link).
  8. Bank sign-in — Sign in to the bank (simulated; demo credentials only).
  9. Connecting — Connecting to the bank (animated).
  10. Bank connected — Bank connected.
  11. Credit authorization (hard pull) — Final credit authorization (hard pull).
  12. Processing — Loader while the application is processed.
  13. Choose funding account — Approved: choose the account the money goes to.
  14. Sign the agreement — Review and sign the loan agreement.
  15. Funded — Funded confirmation: the money has been sent, disclosures, portal login.
